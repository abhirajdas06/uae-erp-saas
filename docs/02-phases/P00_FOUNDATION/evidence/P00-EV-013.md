- `E_INVOICING -> INVOICING` as a hard dependency.

- `SALES -> CRM` as optional/conditional.
- `VAT -> ACCOUNTING`, `FIXED_ASSETS -> ACCOUNTING`, and `BANKING -> ACCOUNTING` as conditional.

No listed target points back to its source, and no direct or indirect cycle exists in the proposed matrix. Platform prerequisites point from business modules toward shared platform capabilities and do not create reciprocal commercial dependencies.
